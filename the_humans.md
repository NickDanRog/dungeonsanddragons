# HUMAN ETHNITICES IN ARTAXARTA

## Regional Peoples
These are people native to the region near Artaxarta. People of these ethnicities are either native to the region or are recent immigrants - most people who spend a more than a few generations here tend to assimilate into Artaxartoi culture, something it excels at. 

### Astani
Descended from the native horse riders of the great Astani plain, the Astani are a proud and fierce people. In the current age they are prinarly found in the Kingdom of Asta and the Principate of Morna which border Artaxarta, and the much larger Grand Duchy of Asta further to the east, which encompasses great swaths of farms and grasslands ruled over by squabbling regional nobles. Astani are fiercely competitive in all aspects of their lives, finding joy in every victory and looking to revenge every slight or defeat. This has led to a strong tradition of dueling in Astani cultures. Physically they are smaller and slighter than average with pale skin and dark hair and eyes. 

Astani Male Names: Antonio, Guiseppe, Lorenzo, Salvatore, Tommaso 
Astani Female Names: Aurora, Bianca, Lucia, Sofia, Valentina
Astani Surnames: Caruso, Colombino, Esposito, Marino, Rossi
