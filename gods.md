## Altair
Altair, also called Astra, is the god of stars and a patron of travelers. He has many powers of prophecy, and in ancient times had many different oracles. He is said to still inspire people to prophecy, though his powers drive some insane. The mad or mentally ill may find shelter at his temple as he has a special love for these people and for all the helpless. In addition to the power of prophecy, he is also said to be a great magician, and the prohibition against this art in many places makes his worship somewhat unpopular. Being associated with the movement of heavenly bodies he is also a methodical deity, and as such is a patron of accountants and mathematicians. His cult is mysterious, with few public ceremonies, and its secret rituals remain hidden to this day. In battle, he appears as a shrouded figure wielding a staff of incredible magic power. His worship is primarily centered in and around the kingdom bearing his name, though he has small secret groups of adherents almost everywhere. 

## Arluna
Arluna, or Selene, is the powerful goddess of the moons and the ruler of the night. She is said to have a palace in each of the moons, and lives in which ever one is brighter at the time. She rules the night, and thieves will often pray to her when venturing out under her gaze. She is a patroness of women in general, but more especially of powerful women. She is said to be fond of tricks as well, and is constantly playing them on the other gods. She takes special joy in poking fun at her older sister Uriel, the sea goddess. She is a sorceress, but only makes use of dark magics when both moons are dark, as during this time all her other powers fail. She is also known as a huntress, and as such is a patroness of both hunters and wild animals who hunt in the night. The few times she is encountered in the world she appears as a beautiful pale huntress armed with a bow and a long knife. When in battle she appears in this form as well, and is attended by a pack of vicious dogs as black in color as to be almost invisible, or a single huge black panther. She is greatly worshiped in the Selune, which is her holy city. She also has an important temple in Dione. 

## Comar
Comar is the god of darkness, and the embodiment of the evil in men’s souls. Though a dark and rather evil deity, he is not without his own sense of justice and is seen as a protector of those seeking revenge. Many that pray to him do so to seek protection from his powers. He does not appear often to men, and is said to do so only to inspire murder or suffering. He is a patron of assassins and torturers. Though one of the chief deities in the pantheon of the world, his worship is outlawed many places due to the darkness it inspires in its followers. Kal-Duras is one of the few places where there is a temple erected to him. He appears as a handsome man with dark hair and a long dark beard, and his eyes are black pits. In battle he wears heavy black plate armor and carries a huge battle axe covered in blood. 

## Ilura
Ilura is the goddess of love, and is the force of elemental light. She inspires not only romantic love, but also the love of home and family. She is seen as a protector of households, and many of her adherents have retained the custom of keeping a small carved figure of her on their mantle or hearth since ancient times. Her cult is perhaps the most prolific in the Council-Lands that make up the former Empire, being almost universally worshiped in one form or another. She is a patron of artists, and is said to inspire all forms of beauty. She also has many powers of healing, and is a patron of physicians and herbalists. She appears often to men, mostly in dreams, and is depicted often as a beautiful, fair, voluptuous woman. In battle, she is depicted carrying a golden bow, and her arrows are said to turn her enemies against each other. She is also a protector of women and children. She has no main center of worship; rather she has many important temples throughout the world. 

## Kai
Kai is the sun god, and the ruler of the day. He is a radiant and powerful deity, and though he interacts often with men, he always seems to be aloof from them in many respects. He also has some powers of prophecy, but unlike others, he speaks his divinations in metaphor and prose rather than acting through signs and prodigies. He is a healer and a teacher, and is said to have invented poetry and music. He is said to be impossible to gaze directly upon. He is a patron of artists and doctors. In battle, he carries a mirror-like shield that focuses the power of the sun into a devastating and powerful beam. He also sometimes appears as a lion, and these animals are sacred to him. He has no earthly house, rather he moves across the sky in the chariot of the sun, driven by a team of twenty flying horses. It is said one can see his face by looking at the top of his shining temple in Ashtar on the summer solstice. He is worshiped all over the known world, but epically in Ashtar and Dione.

## Kary
Kary, sometimes called Kylani or Sosti, is a fire goddess. She is depicted as having the upper body and head of a beautiful woman, and the lower body of a serpent, though she sometimes takes a fully human form. In either form, she is usually depicted as having jet back hair, and eyes the glow with intense flame. Though quick to anger, she is also a civilizing goddess. She gives men the gift of fire and is a patron of all forms of invention and artifice, being especially fond of both blacksmiths and inventors. In battle she carries a sword made of flames and is shown riding on a fire-breathing dragon. She is said to live in the depths of the massive Sosti Volcano deep in the Barrier Mountains. There is no main center of her worship, and she is not highly venerated by any one particular people, though many of the more tribal cultures in the north favor her. 

## Kaelhest
Kaelhest, sometimes called Kael or Kal, is the god of war. He is an ancient deity, and is known to all peoples of the world in one form or another. He represents the ritual aspect of warfare, and is wrathful and full of battle-lust. Though warlike, he is by no means an unintelligent fighter. Those who worship him study all the forms of warfare and its arts, and skill in battle is celebrated in his followers as much as victory. He is a patron of soldiers and warriors everywhere. Many people in lands outside the federation say that this god was thrown down centuries ago and replaced by a similar deity some call Khil among many other names. The Kala’Nari in particular espouse this belief, and worship this new deity almost exclusively. Khil is considered to be less restrained and more vicious than his predecessor. A great struggle continues to take place among the temples of this deity as to which is the true god as of this writing, though the Temple of the Twelve Divines denies the existence of the newer deity. Kaelhest is depicted as a tall, strong, handsome warrior usually with decidedly Durasi features. He wears burnished steel plate armor, and is always seen carrying at least five different weapons. Khil is depicted in a similar fashion, though always carrying a heavy two-handed sword and with more decidedly Kala’Nari features.

## Kozar
Kozar, or alternatively Kozan or Kozen in some regions, is a deity chiefly concerned with the elemental force of Air and the winds. He calls the four winds, which are depicted in myth as his sometimes unruly children. He is a weather deity, having control over storms and rain. In battle he wields a spear that can take the form of a bolt of lightning, and his crown is made of storm cloud. He is also known as a somewhat mercurial deity; his moods are hard to gauge and his wroth is devastating. His eyes flash with lighting when he is angered. He is also a patron of sailors and farmers. His favorite animal is the hawk. He is associated strongly with wild lands, and is venerated greatly by nomads and adventurers. He is said to dwell near top of Mount Kratakos, a lonely peak which juts up in the grasslands of Kesh. He is worshiped greatly in the east, though many in the northern border marches favor him as well. 

## Maximus
Maximus is name taken by the deified Simon Duras, legendary warrior king and founder of the Durasi dynasty and the Empire. Though he lived thousands of years ago, he is still remember fondly by his people today, and is one of the most venerated of deities. He is a god of justice, of warfare, and of ruler-ship. He is a patron of judges, kings, and leaders of all kinds. He is also a patron of diplomats, philosophers, and is a protector of soldiers. His aspect of war is chiefly concerned with strategy, organization, and the soldier’s code of honor. He is also a god of promises and pledges, and oaths sworn on his name are considered the most sacred. He is usually depicted as being accompanied by a golden eagle perched on his shoulder, and wears equipment almost identical to that worn by the Imperial Guards into battle. He is also attended in myth by many other famous relatives and heroes, chiefly by his brother Noman, who serves him as a spirit of divine retribution in redress for his attempt to kill him. He is worshiped all over the lands of the former empire, though peoples in the north associate him with a deity known as Tyr, who is an ancient god of justice native to those regions. In Kesh, he is strongly associated with that country’s hero-founder Rei’inam Mulistinabi, and Keshite scholars have argued for thousands of years as to weather or not they are or were the same person. The historical Mulistinabi lived and died about five hundred years before Simon Duras.
 

## Mora
Mora (or Morna) is a goddess of wisdom and civilization. She is a city-goddess, and is linked in many ways with the earliest civilized cities of the heartlands and the Pontic Coast.  It was she who taught men reading, writing, and the sciences. She is credited with inventing philosophy and with writing the first book. This book is said to contain all knowledge, and she carries it with her whenever she is seen or depicted. She also carries a magic wand with her that makes things change their shape, and changes people’s thoughts. She is a patron of scholars and teachers, and also of magicians. Though she practices sorcery, she is still loved by many people who hate these arts, and is said to always use her powers only for the good of men. She is often seen attended by many birds, especially owls. She is perhaps the deity most intimately associated with mankind, and she is known to move often among them. In addition to her powers of magic, she is also a war goddess, though her warrior aspect is more concerned with strategy and cunning than the love of battle like her brother Kael. She is revered all over the world, but is perhaps most celebrated in Xosha as she is said to have taught these people their beloved democracy. She is also greatly loved greatly by the Tarboni and the Durasi.

## Quoros
Quoros, or Kuros, is a deity of elemental earth, and is also concerned with agriculture. He, like his sister Mora, is a civilizing influence on man, teaching him how to grow crops and set stones together in times beyond history. He is celebrated for his influence in the world, and his long history in involvement in the affairs of men. He is a god of green growing things, and is a great lover of animals, especially those close to the earth; snakes and bears in particular. He is depicted as a giant of a man with a huge beard with twigs and vines wound through it. He is a patron of miners, builders, and farmers. He is also said to be fond of vintners, and is known to drink great quantities of wine. He is also a somewhat mischievous deity, and is known for his fondness of tricks. He rarely engages in battle, preferring to manipulate his enemies into destroying themselves. When he does fight, he is depicted as wielding a great hammer made of stone.  His house is said to be directly beneath his ancient temple complex in the city of Antax. He is worshiped everywhere, but most extensively throughout the heartlands region. There are important temples to him in Antax, Tarbon, and Karas. 

## Uriel
Uriel, sometimes called Umma, is a water deity. She has power over the seas, rivers, and lakes of the world, and is said to live deep within the great lake that bears her name. Like her brother Kai, she also possesses many powers having to do with healing. She is usually depicted as having the torso and head of a beautiful human female, and the lower body of a fish or otter. She is considered to be a friendly goddess, and is known for her love of friendly sea and water creatures like dolphins, otters, and whales. She often falls prey to mood swings however, especially when she is fighting with her younger sister Arluna, and will sometimes visit disaster on those who do not appease her. She, like her brother Kozar, is a patroness to sailors, and many of these people perform a ritual sacrifice to her. She is also a patroness of fishermen and those who live along rivers or coastlines. In battle, she carries a silver trident that has the appearance of being made of solid water, and is escorted by a school of gigantic sharks. The people in the regions around Lake Uriel venerate her greatly, though she also has several major temples in the cities along the Long Coast.
