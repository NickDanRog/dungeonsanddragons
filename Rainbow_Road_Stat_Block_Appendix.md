# Asura Types

The creatures called "demons" by the people of the Nine Worlds are technically Asuras — outsiders born from divine mistakes, exiles from Hell who infiltrated the Nine Worlds during their construction. The goodness of the mortal populations rejected these entities, and the celestials responded by forming the Underworld as a prison. The asuras' lingering influence has plagued the worlds throughout their history, and the current crisis began when the ten heroes' attempt to sever the connection to the Underworld backfired, allowing demons to physically enter the worlds for the first time.

All asuras share the following racial traits unless otherwise noted in their individual entries:

**Asura Subtype Traits:**
- **Immunity** to curse effects, disease, and poison
- **Resistance** to acid 10 and electricity 10
- **Telepathy** 100 ft. (CR 5+)
- **Spell Resistance** equal to 11 + CR (CR 5+)
- **Regeneration** or **Fast Healing** (overcome by good weapons and good spells)
- **Damage Reduction** /cold iron or good (low CR) or /good (mid-high CR)
- **Elusive Aura (Su):** Asuras and their possessions are treated as though under the effects of a *nondetection* spell. The radius and DC scale with CR.
- **Spell-Like Abilities:** All asuras of CR 5+ have *greater teleport* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only) at will. Most have a *summon* ability (1/day) that calls other asuras.
- **+6 racial bonus to Escape Artist, +4 racial bonus to Perception**
- **Bonus vs. Enchantment:** +2 racial bonus on saves against enchantment spells and effects

### Tripurasura
*CR 2 — Tiny — "Gremlin"*

The most common asura encountered in the Nine Worlds. Tripurasuras are infiltrators and spies that assume humanoid forms to corrupt mortals and undermine faith. ### Beast Blavin
*CR 5 — Large — Casomir's Corrupted Form*

The Beast Blavin is the corrupted manifestation of Casomir's mythic power, forced to the surface when the last of his control fails. What emerges is not a separate entity but a distortion of Casomir himself — his body enlarged, twisted, and driven by the corruption that has been consuming him for five centuries. The transformation progresses through three phases as Casomir's resistance erodes and the Beast takes full control. The creature has no intelligence of its own; it is instinct wrapped in corrupted flesh, acting on impulse and rage. Casomir remains partially present during the early stages of the transformation, fighting the Beast from within and providing what aid he can to the party before his consciousness is submerged entirely.
