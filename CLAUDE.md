## Standing Rules — Broken Wheel

**Special Recruitment** means a unit is available only through special campaign events in the adventure. Do not ask whether there is a regional restriction or other condition — there is none beyond the special event trigger.

**Elite Unit** means a maximum of 1 of that unit per faction. This is marked on the entry and needs no further clarification.

**Unit design questions:** Make a judgment call using existing entries as precedent, then write. Do not ask whether to proceed — the user will correct anything that needs adjustment.

**Tactics** listed in a unit entry as "starting — innate" do not count against the earned-tactic maximum (ACR ÷ 2). Earned tactics are capped separately.

**HP formula:** ACR × average HD value (d6: ×3.5 / d8: ×4.5 / d10: ×5.5 / d12: ×6.5), rounded down.

**Committing:** Do not commit or push after edits. Wait for explicit instruction.

**"Demons" (Broken Wheel):** "Demons" are PF1e Daemons. Use Daemon creature type and Daemon stat blocks in GM-facing text. "Demon" is in-world colloquial only.

## Standing Rules — All Campaigns

**Rainbow Road:** "Demons" are PF1e Asuras. Use Asura stat blocks. "Demon" is in-world colloquial only. Read-aloud (blockquote) text uses "demon"; GM-facing text uses "Asura."

**Stat block appendices:** Write unique creature stat block appendix entries at the time the chapter is written — do not defer.

**NPC names in DM Guide front matter:** Antagonists described by role/faction only in introductory sections. Named characters belong in chapters.

**The Realm sections:** No adventure-specific details (scene mechanics, decision points, plot beats) in The Realm. Those belong in Running the Adventure and chapter text.

**No emoji** in any campaign document or response.

**Commits:** Only commit when explicitly asked.

## Clarifying Questions Policy

Ask before writing only when:
- A design decision has two or more plausible interpretations with meaningfully different mechanical consequences, AND
- The existing rules or entries provide no clear precedent

Do not ask about:
- How to stat a unit — use the formula and existing entries as precedent
- Whether Special Recruitment has a geographic restriction — it does not
- What an "Elite Unit" means — limit 1 per faction
- Format or structure questions where an existing entry already demonstrates the pattern
